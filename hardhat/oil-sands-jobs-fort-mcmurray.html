Oil Sands Jobs in Fort McMurray With No Experience

Fort Mac is where the camp jobs are. Suncor, CNRL and Imperial's Kearl mine all run FIFO camps out here. Most entry hiring — labourers, ground workers, safety watch — happens through contractors and staffing firms during turnaround season, not on LinkedIn. Here's the exact tickets, who's hiring, and what the rotations and pay really look like.

The big employers (and how you actually get on)
  • Suncor — Base Plant, Fort Hills, Firebag. Runs camps; entry hiring often via contractors.
  • Canadian Natural (CNRL) — Horizon & Albian. High-volume mine + upgrader work.
  • Imperial (Kearl) — one of the largest oil-sands mines; ground-worker and operator entry roles.

You rarely get hired straight off the producer's careers page with no experience — you get in through the staffing firms and site contractors that crew these projects. That's the door.

Rotations & camp life

Common patterns are 14/7 (14 days on, 7 off) and 6/6. You fly in, live in camp (room + meals covered), work 12-hour shifts, then fly home fully off. It's real money for real time away — and the pay reflects the lifestyle.

The tickets to book before you apply
  • H2S Alive — mandatory for site access. 1 day, valid 3 years.
  • CSO (Common Safety Orientation) or CSTS — online, ~CAD 90, doesn't expire.
  • Standard First Aid & CPR-C.
  • Pass the pre-access drug & alcohol test — the step that catches people out.

Pay (CAD, illustrative — verify on the posting)

Entry labourer / ground worker roughly $28–$37/hr; heavy-equipment operators reach $54–$58/hr with experience. Present these as ranges — oil-sands hiring spikes in turnaround/shutdown season (spring & fall) and softens between, so apply ahead of it and expect competition.

Reality check: job counts on the boards are modest (dozens, not thousands) and some roles now want real experience. The winners get their tickets sorted, get on agency books early, and are ready the moment turnaround hiring opens.
