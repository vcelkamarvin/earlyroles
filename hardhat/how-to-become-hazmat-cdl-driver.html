The route in, step by step

  1. Get the accredited tickets. Hazmat / CDL Haul runs on safety certification — the required ones are listed below with real training providers.
  2. Pass the medical. Nearly every role needs a valid fitness-to-work medical.
  3. Build an offshore-format CV. Tickets, medicals and reliability first — not a white-collar layout.
  4. Apply through the right channel. These jobs come from crewing agencies and operator talent pools (e.g. Aerotek / Actalent, Roehl Transport), not general job boards.

FAQ

How much does a hazmat / CDL driver earn?

Entry pay is around $72k and experienced workers reach $135k. Oilfield & hazmat pays a premium over dry van.

Can I become a hazmat / CDL driver with no experience?

Yes. Hazmat / CDL Haul has genuine entry routes — you qualify on accredited safety tickets and a medical, not on prior experience or a degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: CDL Class A, Hazmat + Tanker endorsement, DOT Medical Card. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
