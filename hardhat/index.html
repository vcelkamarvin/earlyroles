The real process

How offshore hiring actually works

These jobs don’t come from LinkedIn. Here’s the real route, and we walk you through every step of it.

1

Get accredited tickets

Book your mandatory safety courses from accredited providers — OPITO for BOSIET, the Global Wind Organisation for GWO, and so on. We link you straight to them.

2

Pass the medical

An offshore/OGUK or equivalent fitness medical plus a drug & alcohol screen. Non-negotiable before you set foot on site.

3

Build an offshore CV

Tickets, medicals and reliability go first — not an office CV. Our builder formats it the way rig and vessel recruiters expect.

4

Register with crewing agencies

Get on the books of the specialist agencies and operator talent pools that actually place people offshore. Your shortlist unlocks with Pro.

5

Interview & verify

A short competency chat and document check. We prep you on exactly what they ask.

6

Get placed on rotation

Sign on for your first hitch (2/3, 28/28 or FIFO) and start earning. Travel, camp and meals are often covered.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I really not need a degree?

Correct. Every sector we cover hires on tickets, medicals and attitude — not degrees. Our paid tools get you those tickets faster and in the right order.

Why is applying a paid feature?

Browsing jobs, the pay explorer and your Rig-Ready score are free. Applying — the agency directory, application templates and done-for-you CV — is where we add the most value, so it’s part of HardHat Pro.

Do you place me in a job?

We’re your roadmap and toolkit — the tickets, the matched jobs and the agency contacts. You apply; agencies and operators hire. We make sure you reach them job-ready.

Where do you operate?

Worldwide. Roles span the North Sea, Gulf of Mexico, Middle East, West Africa, Australia, Brazil, Southeast Asia and more.

Are the companies and pay figures real?

The companies shown are real employers active in these sectors; we’re independent and not affiliated with them. Pay ranges and demand figures are illustrative industry estimates to help you plan.
