The route in, step by step

  1. Get the accredited tickets. Offshore Wind runs on safety certification — the required ones are listed below with real training providers.
  2. Pass the medical. Nearly every role needs a valid fitness-to-work medical.
  3. Build an offshore-format CV. Tickets, medicals and reliability first — not a white-collar layout.
  4. Apply through the right channel. These jobs come from crewing agencies and operator talent pools (e.g. Taylor Hopkinson, Airswift Renewables), not general job boards.

Tickets & medicals

  • GWO Basic Safety Training required · $1,200–$1,800 · 4–5 days — Global Wind Organisation

    Mechanical, electrical & hydraulic basics.
  • Offshore Medical (ENG1/OGUK) required · $150–$250 · 1 day — OEUK / ENG1 providers
    Fitness to work offshore.

    Helicopter underwater escape for offshore transfer.

FAQ

How much does a offshore wind technician earn?

Entry pay is around $65k and experienced workers reach $140k. GWO tickets open EU + US offshore wind.

Can I become a offshore wind technician with no experience?

Yes. Offshore Wind has genuine entry routes — you qualify on accredited safety tickets and a medical, not on prior experience or a degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: GWO Basic Safety Training, Offshore Medical (ENG1/OGUK), HUET / sea survival. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
