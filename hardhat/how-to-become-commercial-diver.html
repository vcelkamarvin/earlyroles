The route in, step by step

  1. Get the accredited tickets. Commercial Diving runs on safety certification — the required ones are listed below with real training providers.
  2. Pass the medical. Nearly every role needs a valid fitness-to-work medical.
  3. Build an offshore-format CV. Tickets, medicals and reliability first — not a white-collar layout.
  4. Apply through the right channel. These jobs come from crewing agencies and operator talent pools (e.g. Faststream, Subsea 7 careers), not general job boards.

FAQ

How much does a commercial diver earn?

Entry pay is around $65k and experienced workers reach $250k. Saturation diving is the top of the pay scale.

Can I become a commercial diver with no experience?

It usually requires trade training or certification first — see the required tickets below — but no university degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: Commercial Dive School, Diver Medical. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
