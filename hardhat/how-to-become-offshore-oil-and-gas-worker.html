The route in, step by step

  1. Get the accredited tickets. Offshore Oil & Gas runs on safety certification — the required ones are listed below with real training providers.
  2. Pass the medical. Nearly every role needs a valid fitness-to-work medical.
  3. Build an offshore-format CV. Tickets, medicals and reliability first — not a white-collar layout.
  4. Apply through the right channel. These jobs come from crewing agencies and operator talent pools (e.g. Airswift, Orion Group, NES Fircroft), not general job boards.

Tickets & medicals

  • BOSIET / OPITO required · $900–$1,400 · 3 days — OPITO
    Offshore survival & sea-escape (HUET). Non-negotiable to step on a rig.
  • OGUK / OEUK Medical required · $150–$250 · 1 day — OEUK / OGUK medical providers
    Offshore fitness medical, valid 2 years.
  • MIST required · $120–$200 · 1 day — OPITO (MIST)
    Minimum Industry Safety Training induction.
  • TWIC card (US) · $125 · 2–4 wks — TSA Universal Enrollment
    Transport Worker ID — needed for US Gulf platforms.
  • Rig Pass / SafeGulf · $50–$150 · 1 day — SafeGulf / SafeLandUSA
    US onshore/offshore safety orientation.

FAQ

How much does a offshore oil & gas worker earn?

Entry pay is around $70k and experienced workers reach $165k. Day-rate roles; overtime common offshore.

Can I become a offshore oil & gas worker with no experience?

Yes. Offshore Oil & Gas has genuine entry routes — you qualify on accredited safety tickets and a medical, not on prior experience or a degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: BOSIET / OPITO, OGUK / OEUK Medical, MIST. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
