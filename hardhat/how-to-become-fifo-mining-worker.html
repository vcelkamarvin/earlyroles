The route in, step by step

  1. Get the accredited tickets. FIFO Mining (Canada, US & AU) runs on safety certification — the required ones are listed below with real training providers.
  2. Pass the medical. Nearly every role needs a valid fitness-to-work medical.
  3. Build an offshore-format CV. Tickets, medicals and reliability first — not a white-collar layout.
  4. Apply through the right channel. These jobs come from crewing agencies and operator talent pools (e.g. Hays Mining, WorkPac, Airswift (Canada), The Bouchier Group, PTW Energy Services), not general job boards.

FAQ

How much does a FIFO mining worker earn?

Entry pay is around $70k and experienced workers reach $180k. Canada (oil sands), US & Australia. Camp, flights & meals usually covered on top.

Can I become a FIFO mining worker with no experience?

Yes. FIFO Mining (Canada, US & AU) has genuine entry routes — you qualify on accredited safety tickets and a medical, not on prior experience or a degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: Site Safety Induction, Pre-employment Medical + D&A. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
