Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. HardHat helps people find high-paying offshore and trades work.

What we collect. The information you enter in the Rig-Ready assessment (sector interest, experience, tickets, availability and contact details) and your account email. In this build, your profile, roadmap progress and saved jobs are stored locally in your browser.

How we use it. To build your roadmap, match you to jobs, and — if you opt in — send job alerts. We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties. Payments are handled by Stripe. Optional AI features send only the text you submit to our AI provider to generate a result.

Your control. You can clear your data at any time by clearing your browser storage, or contact us at hello@hardhatjobs.co.
