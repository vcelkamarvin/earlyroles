FAQ

How much does a wind turbine technician earn?

Entry pay is around $58k and experienced workers reach $105k. Traveling techs get per-diem on top.

Can I become a wind turbine technician with no experience?

Yes. Wind Turbine Tech has genuine entry routes — you qualify on accredited safety tickets and a medical, not on prior experience or a degree.

What tickets do I need to start?

The mandatory ones are: GWO Working at Heights + First Aid, Climb Medical. Training-provider links are listed above, free.
